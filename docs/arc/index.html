Fuel on Arc: governed agent payments on Arc mainnet

Fuel is a wallet and spend-control layer for AI agents. Here, one agent pays for items in real USDC on Arc mainnet, and every payment passes Fuel's controls first: spend caps, a hold for unusual amounts that a person must approve, a rate limit, a freeze switch, and a ledger of every attempt.

Who is who. The agent, its wallet, the shop and the shop's wallet are all mine (the founder of Fuel). Every payment below moves my own USDC from my agent's wallet to my shop's wallet. The shop is a three-item demo I operate so the controls have something real to pay for. No customer funds are involved.

How the lock works

  • Exactly one agent may pay on Arc. It is named in code by its id, its company's id and its wallet address. Every other agent and company in Fuel stays on test money (Base Sepolia), with every testnet guard unchanged.
  • That agent's signer signs only a USDC transfer on Arc, from its own wallet, to my shop's wallet, of at most $0.01, and only while its lifetime total stays at or under $5.00.
  • The shop settles each payment itself, submitting the signed transfer to Arc, and its settler can submit nothing else.
  • An automated test suite (scripts/verify-arc-lock.ts, run on every change) checks all of the above. Before going live, each part of the lock was also deliberately broken, one way at a time, and each break was caught by its own check.
